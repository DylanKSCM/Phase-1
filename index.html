Automation

Unattended back-office report pipeline

A dealer portal had no scheduled exports, so a dozen operational reports (inventory, aged stock, purchasing, sales, rebates, transfers, cash) were pulled by hand.

  • AI browser agent on a schedule drives the portal's report screens, fills complex custom dropdowns and date pickers, and collects finished files from the queue
  • Files land in dated folders with consistent names; month-to-date reports are prioritized for downstream consumers
  • Hard safety stops: never enters credentials; sends a phone push alert on any block it can't clear
  • Runbooks plus ~100 structured "lessons learned" notes keep the agent reliable over time

Data quality

Integrity guardrails for automated feeds

Automated pulls fail quietly: partial coverage, stale copies, or cloud sync making an old file look new.

  • 15-minute gap detector that trusts the timestamp inside each workbook, not the file's modified time
  • Post-run validator that confirms every location is present in the output
  • SHA-256 de-duplication of identical hourly copies (freed ~300 MB in a day)
  • Recovery for stuck downloads, safe cleanup, and a missed-day back-fill tool

Analytics

Accessory margin dashboard

Leadership had no single view of margin, attach rate, inventory turn, and end-of-life exposure.

  • pandas ETL joins monthly sales and inventory exports to a product catalog and store hierarchy (~99.7% catalog match)
  • Output is one offline HTML file: month selector, hierarchy filters, drill-down tables, opportunity finder, CSV export
  • Per-rep scorecard and adjustable margin thresholds

Alerting

Config-driven inventory alert emailer

Field leaders needed a short, prioritized view of stockouts and overstock in their area.

  • Modular CLI: loader → layered rules engine → HTML email renderer → sender
  • Thresholds cascade from default to category, tier, model, and store; unassigned stores roll up a level
  • Emits a manifest a Power Automate flow sends from in one run; dry-run by default, with tests
